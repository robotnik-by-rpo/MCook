### Description
My project is a telegram bot. If you have leftover ingredients and you don't know what to cook today, my bot will help you. You can point your range price and write in Telegram about your meal and products which you have, and bot will offer 2 dishes for you.

### Monetization
Users without a subscription have a limits on numbers of requests to the bot. 
Users with a subscription have no limits.
A monthly subscription costs 50 Telegram Stars(~67 rubles).

### Target audience
People who don't know what cook. This can be a poor student, a worker or a rich person.

### Relevance
We live in fast world. People try to spend less time on cooking. Most people spend a lot of time trying to figure out what they can cook from the ingredients they have. My bot solves this problems. You just need to write 2 sentances and you will get recipes.
